General Experience and Reflections

What was the most challenging part of this project for me? Was I able to overcome it?

The most challenging part of the project for me was doing the map pin on the home page of the site I was a Lead Dev for. I figured out how to get it to work in the end with a grid display and relative values instead of pixel values.

What did I enjoy most about this project?

I enjoyed the CSS most in this project, I find it satisfying to create something beautiful for a site.

What would I do differently if I could do the project again?

I would probably space out my work more, so I had more to do throughout the project time instead of getting it all done in the first week.

Do I feel more confident in working on real-world projects after this experience? Explain.

I feel more confident in working on real-world projects after this experience. I was able to make my own personal site with all the information I learned from this class, and I do love how it looks and behaves.
